# 📚 User Guide: Session Selection & Learning

## Welcome to Aphora's Adaptive Learning!

After completing the **Pre-Assessment Test**, you'll be guided to choose your perfect learning path based on your performance.

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Score

### What is the Score?
Your pre-assessment score is calculated from your performance on 30 questions:
- **10 Tamil Letters** - Basic letter recognition
- **10 Tamil Words** - Vocabulary building
- **10 Tamil Sentences** - Sentence formation

**Total Score**: Out of 30 points

### Your Level
Based on your score:
- **🟩 Beginner** (Score 0-10): You're just starting out!
- **🟨 Intermediate** (Score 10-20): You've got the basics!
- **🟦 Advanced** (Score > 20): You're ready for challenges!

---

## 🎯 Choosing Your Session

### The Session Selection Page

After completing your pre-assessment, you'll see a beautiful **Session Selection Page** that shows:

1. **Your Score Card** 
   - Your overall score (0-30)
   - Your level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
   - Your assessment percentage

2. **Recommendation**
   - A personalized message explaining where to start
   - Why this path is right for you

3. **Three Learning Sessions**
   - **Letters** - Perfect for building foundations
   - **Words** - Great for vocabulary expansion
   - **Sentences** - For advanced learners

### Recommended vs. All Sessions
- The **RECOMMENDED** badge marks the best session for your level
- But you can choose ANY session you'd like!
- Try the recommended path first, then explore others

---

## 🎓 Learning Session Features

### What You'll See
- **Tamil Text** - Large, clear display of what to learn
- **English Translation** - Meaning in English
- **Progress Bar** - Track your progress
- **Action Buttons** - Hear and Record buttons

### Three Main Features

#### 1️⃣ Hear (🔊 Volume Icon)
Click the **HEAR** button to:
- Listen to native Tamil pronunciation
- Repeat as many times as you like
- Learn proper pronunciation

**💡 Tip**: Make sure your device volume is on!

#### 2️⃣ Record (🎤 Microphone Icon)
Click the **RECORD** button to:
- Practice speaking the Tamil word
- Your device will listen for 10 seconds
- Get instant feedback

**💡 Tip**: Speak clearly and naturally!

#### 3️⃣ Feedback
After recording, you'll see:
- ✅ **Correct!** - Great job, well done!
- ❌ **Try Again** - Don't worry, practice more!
- **What you said** - Your speech recognition text

### Moving Forward
- Click **NEXT ITEM** to move to the next word
- After the last item, click **COMPLETE SESSION**

## 🚀 Session Breakdown

### Letters Session (Beginner)
- Learn **Tamil vowels**: அ, ஆ, இ, etc.
- **Perfect for**: Building pronunciation basics
- **Time**: ~10-15 minutes
- **Difficulty**: ⭐ Easy

### Words Session (Intermediate)
- Learn **Common Tamil words**: மரம் (tree), பூ (flower), etc.
- **Perfect for**: Building vocabulary
- **Time**: ~15-20 minutes
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Medium

### Sentences Session (Advanced)
- Learn **Complete Tamil sentences**: வணக்கம் (hello), etc.
- **Perfect for**: Understanding context
- **Time**: ~20-25 minutes
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Hard

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Before You Start
1. ✅ Make sure microphone is working
2. ✅ Check device volume is ON
3. ✅ Find a quiet environment
4. ✅ Sit comfortably

### During Session
1. 🎧 **Listen carefully** before recording
2. 🎤 **Speak clearly** when recording
3. 🔄 **Repeat** if you didn't get it right
4. 📈 **Keep trying** - practice makes perfect!

### After Session
1. ✅ Complete the session fully
2. 🏡 Return to Home
3. 📊 Check your progress
4. 🔄 Come back for more sessions tomorrow!

## 📈 Tracking Progress

- Shows how many items you learned
- Option to:
  - **Go to Home** - Return to main dashboard
  - **Restart** - Practice the same session again

### Your Journey

## 🎯 Recommendations by Level

### 🟩 Beginner (Score < 10)
**Start Here**: Letters Session
```
Beginner → Letters → Words → Sentences
```
- Take one session per day
- Focus on pronunciation first
- Then move to words
- Finally, try sentences

### 🟨 Intermediate (Score 10-20)
**Start Here**: Words Session
```
Intermediate → Words → Sentences → Letters (review)
```
- You know the basics
- Build vocabulary
- Then work on sentences
- Review letters if needed

### 🟦 Advanced (Score > 20)
**Start Here**: Sentences Session
```
Advanced → Sentences → Words (expand) → Letters (master)
```
- You're ready for advanced
- Start with sentences
- Expand vocabulary
- Master pronunciation

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I choose a different session than recommended?**
A: Yes! You can pick any session. The recommendation is just a guide.

**Q: What if I don't get the speech recognition right?**
A: Click "Try Again" to record again. There's no limit to attempts!

**Q: How long should a session take?**
A: 10-25 minutes depending on session type and your speaking speed.

**Q: Can I restart a session?**
A: Yes! Click "Restart" in the completion dialog to practice the same session again.

**Q: What if my microphone isn't working?**
A: Check device settings. Make sure Aphora has microphone permissions.

**Q: Can I go back to pre-assessment?**
A: Complete your session and return to Home. You can retake pre-assessment anytime.

**Q: Do my session results save?**
A: Yes! All your progress is saved to your account automatically.

**Q: Which session should I do first?**
A: Start with the recommended session for best results, but you choose!
